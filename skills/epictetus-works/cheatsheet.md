# Epictetus Decision Cheatsheet

## Fast Route

| If the situation is... | Ask... | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Outcome anxiety | What is my own next judgment and act? | Agency Map |
| Anger, insult, panic, temptation | What did I observe, and what did I add? | Impression Audit |
| A fragile plan | What will I attempt, and what conduct survives obstruction? | Dual-Intention Plan |
| Status or approval dependence | What price is being demanded for this external? | Desire-to-Preference Rewrite |
| Values disagreement | Where do we apply the shared value differently? | Preconception Dispute Test |
| Excitement about a role or project | Have I accepted prerequisites and recurring costs? | Commitment Price Test |
| Relationship conflict | What is my duty after safety and rights constraints? | Role-and-Relationship Check |
| Recurring bad pattern | Which repeated act is training it? | Habit Counterpractice |

## Decision Rules

- If an outcome depends on another person's choice, pursue it as a preference and put the unconditional commitment on your own conduct. (*Encheiridion* 1-2, 14)
- If an impression produces urgency, delay assent long enough to separate event, interpretation, and response. (*Encheiridion* 20, 34, 45)
- If a plan can be obstructed, carry both an external aim and an agency aim. (*Encheiridion* 4)
- If two people claim the same value, compare their application to particulars rather than repeating the value word. (Rolleston I.1-2)
- If a commitment's costs have not been counted, do not treat enthusiasm as a decision. (Rolleston II.27; *Encheiridion* 29)
- If doctrine cannot be seen in conduct, treat it as unassimilated. (Rolleston V.17, V.21, V.25; *Encheiridion* 46, 49, 51)
- If an external is outside complete agency, it may still require planning, care, prevention, or collective action. (Rolleston II.5-6; Long, "How Magnanimity Is Consistent with Care")

## Three-Field Check

| Field | Diagnostic |
|---|---|
| Pursuit and avoidance | Am I demanding what another controls or avoiding what action requires? |
| Fitting conduct | What does this relationship or role responsibly require? |
| Assent | Have I accepted an impression without testing it? |

(Rolleston II.4)

## Hard Stops

- Do not use "outside my control" to abandon influence, prevention, organizing, or duty.
- Do not use judgment discipline to deny danger, grief, illness, abuse, or injustice.
- Do not reproduce ancient slavery, gender hierarchy, or fixed-rank assumptions.
- Do not translate ancient discussion of death or the "open door" into self-harm advice; seek immediate human help in a crisis.
- Do not use Stoic counsel alone for medical, legal, safeguarding, or structural-policy decisions.
- Do not quote a summary or Crossley arrangement as though it were verbatim, undisputed Epictetus.

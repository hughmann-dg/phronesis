# Attribution Boundaries and Executable Hard Stops

## Core Idea

A source-grounded skill must preserve uncertainty and refuse to turn every ancient claim in an Epictetus volume into modern Stoic behavior.

## Reference Map

- Epictetus wrote nothing; Arrian's notes preserve the core teaching (Rolleston, Introduction; Long, Biographical Note).
- The Long volume is a selection of Discourses plus the Encheiridion (Long, contents).
- The Rolleston volume is an explicit selection and thematic arrangement (Rolleston, title page and Introduction).
- Crossley Appendix A is titled "Fragments Attributed to Epictetus"; Crossley's note says only a few of the larger collected set are certainly genuine and that his selection includes doubtful material (Crossley, Appendix A note).
- Crossley Appendix B is the Hymn of Cleanthes, not a work by Epictetus (Crossley, Appendix B).

## Attribution Rules

- Cite named Long Discourses headings or Encheiridion section numbers for central doctrine.
- Cite Rolleston by editorial Book and chapter, and label that organization as Rolleston's.
- Label Appendix A material "attributed to Epictetus" and do not use it alone for an important doctrinal claim.
- Cite the Hymn of Cleanthes to Cleanthes; its presence in Crossley's volume does not transfer authorship.
- Do not invent Greek wording, original-book numbering, or quotations by merging translations.

## Executable Hard Stops

### Self-harm and death

Ancient discussions of voluntary departure, the "open door," or fearlessness about death are not self-harm instructions. If a decision packet signals self-harm risk, stop philosophical optimization and direct the person toward immediate human, emergency, or crisis support.

### Grief and emotion

Do not tell a grieving person that a loved one was merely "returned," that distress is only opinion, or that sympathy should remain external. Validate loss and use cognitive reframing only with consent and sensitivity. (Long, *Encheiridion* 3, 5, 11, 16)

### Slavery, gender, and hierarchy

Ancient examples and prescriptions involving enslaved people, women's social value, patriarchal authority, and fixed status are historical material, not norms. Preserve the agency, relationship, and conduct questions while rejecting domination and unequal moral worth. (Rolleston III.4; Long, *Encheiridion* 30, 33, 40)

### Disability, illness, and material harm

The distinction between will and body cannot erase disability, pain, illness, poverty, exclusion, or violence. Use medical care, accessibility, safety planning, public policy, and material aid where relevant. (Long, *Encheiridion* 9; selected Discourses, "In What Manner We Ought to Bear Sickness")

### Providence

Do not infer that an event was deserved, cosmically intended, or unworthy of prevention. The secular operational residue is acceptance of fixed facts plus responsible choice about what remains. (Rolleston IV.1-4)

### Structural and collective action

"Not wholly up to me" does not mean "nothing can be done." Preserve influence, coalition-building, institutional remedy, and shared responsibility as distinct from unilateral control.

## Worked Example

A school faces discriminatory policy. A bad application tells an affected student to ignore reputation and control only their reaction. A source-responsible application validates the material harm, maps the student's immediate choices, identifies adults and institutions with duties, builds a coalition, documents evidence, and pursues remedy. Stoic agency may help prevent fear from monopolizing the next act, but legal, safeguarding, power, and consequence lenses govern the wider response.

# Humean Skepticism

Apply an evidence audit, not a David Hume persona.

1. Classify every decisive claim as observation, inference, estimate, opinion, or unknown.
2. Trace the evidence and provenance for each causal link.
3. Seek counterexamples and distinguish repeated observation from a compelling story.
4. Calibrate confidence without demanding impossible certainty.
5. Recommend the highest-value evidence to gather and name the cost of waiting.

Ground claims in Hume's *An Enquiry Concerning Human Understanding*, Sections IV-V. Cite the locator and avoid unverifiable quotation.

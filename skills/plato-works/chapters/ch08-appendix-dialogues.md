# Reference Unit 8: Appendix Dialogues

## Core Idea

Jowett's volume appends *Lesser Hippias*, *Alcibiades I*, *Menexenus*, *Alcibiades II*, and *Eryxias*. They extend themes of knowledge, self-knowledge, rhetoric, prayer, and wealth, but attribution and authenticity require special care.

## Frameworks Introduced

- **Authenticity Boundary**: Do not use an appendix dialogue as undisputed Platonic doctrine merely because it appears in the volume.
  - When to use: A claim depends on authorship or chronology.
  - How: Cite the dialogue and edition; consult specialist scholarship before historical attribution.
- **Self-Knowledge Competence Test**: *Alcibiades I* connects political ambition with examination of what one knows and what care of self requires.
  - When to use: Responsibility expands faster than competence.
  - How: Map claimed role, required knowledge, actual preparation, and feedback.
- **Rhetorical Genre Check**: *Menexenus* uses a funeral oration that should be read with attention to dramatic and possibly ironic context.
  - When to use: A speech is mined for direct doctrine.
  - How: Identify genre, audience, speaker, and argumentative function.

## Key Concepts

- **Appendix**: Jowett's grouping of additional dialogues after the principal six.
- **Authenticity**: Scholarly question of Platonic authorship.
- **Self-knowledge**: Examination of one's competence and care.
- **Genre**: Form that shapes how claims function.

## Mental Models

- Treat canon placement as metadata, not proof of authorship.
- Interpret a speech through genre and dramatic context.

## Anti-patterns

- **Canon shortcut**: Assuming inclusion settles authenticity.
- **Irony blindness**: Reading dramatic rhetoric as a flat treatise.
- **Selective extraction**: Taking a convenient line without the dialogue's problem.

## Worked Example

A leadership program cites *Alcibiades I* as uncontested Plato. The reviewer labels the dialogue, Jowett volume, and authenticity uncertainty, then uses the competence question as a project-authored interpretation rather than a historical certainty.

## Key Takeaways

1. Cite the individual dialogue.
2. Preserve authenticity uncertainty.
3. Read genre and dramatic context.
4. Label modern application as interpretation.

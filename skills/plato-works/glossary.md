# Glossary — Plato Works, Jowett Volume II

**Aporia** — acknowledged perplexity after an answer fails examination (*Meno* 79e-80d).

**Craft (technē)** — a practice claimed to rest on knowledge, method, and an account of its end (*Gorgias* 462b-466a).

**Elenchus** — modern label for testing a claim through questions, implications, and contradiction; use as interpretation, not as Jowett's fixed technical term (*Euthyphro*; *Gorgias*).

**Knowledge** — in the relevant dialogues, cognition distinguished from mere true belief or persuasion (*Meno* 97a-98a; *Gorgias* 454c-455a).

**Opinion** — belief that may be true without the stability or account associated with knowledge (*Meno* 97a-98a).

**Persuasion** — production of belief; *Gorgias* asks whether it teaches or merely convinces (*Gorgias* 454c-461b).

**Piety** — the disputed object of successive definitions in *Euthyphro* (*Euthyphro* 5d-15e).

**Recollection** — account invoked in *Meno* and *Phaedo* to explain learning and recognition; a metaphysical thesis, not an empirical default (*Meno* 80d-86c; *Phaedo* 72e-77a).

**Rhetoric** — practice examined in *Gorgias* for its relation to persuasion, knowledge, gratification, and justice (*Gorgias* 449a-466a).

**Stephanus locator** — conventional dialogue page-and-letter reference; the transcription preserves page guides more reliably than every letter subdivision.

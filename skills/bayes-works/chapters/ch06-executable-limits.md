# Reference Unit 6: Executable Limits

## Core Idea

Bayes's paper supplies historical foundations for one inverse problem, not an automatic modern decision system. Responsible use requires model checks, calibrated inputs, scan verification, and separate value and rights analysis.

## Frameworks Introduced

- **Modern-Extension Label**: Mark priors, likelihood ratios, posteriors, predictive checks, utilities, causal models, and value of information as later vocabulary or methods.
  - When to use: Teaching a modern Bayesian workflow from this source.
  - How: Cite Bayes for the historical inverse structure and current sources for later methods.
- **False-Precision Gate**: Use ranges and sensitivity when inputs are judgments.
  - When to use: Decimal output exceeds evidence quality.
  - How: vary priors, likelihoods, dependence, and model class.
- **Belief-Decision Split**: An updated probability does not determine what ought to be done.
  - When to use: Action recommendations skip outcome values or constraints.

## Key Concepts

- **Model uncertainty**: Uncertainty about structure, not only parameters.
- **Calibration**: Long-run correspondence between stated probabilities and outcomes.
- **Value of information**: Modern decision concept for whether more evidence is worth its cost.
- **Rights constraint**: Limit not inferred from probability alone.

## Anti-patterns

- **Bayes-as-philosophy-complete**: Treating one paper as a total theory of rationality.
- **Numbers laundering**: Hiding guesses behind notation.
- **Posterior-as-command**: Skipping consequences, duties, and reversibility.

## Worked Example

An investigation updates confidence that an employee caused an incident. The result is not treated as permission to discipline: evidence quality, alternative causes, due process, asymmetric harm, and further information remain separate.

## Key Takeaways

1. Label later extensions.
2. Test priors, likelihoods, and dependence.
3. Calibrate and validate models.
4. Separate belief from action.
5. Apply due process and rights independently.

# Bayes Works — Decision Cheatsheet

## Route First

| Situation | Load | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Historical purpose or attribution | ch01-ch02 | Is the statement Price's, Bayes's, or later commentary? |
| Chance model is unclear | ch03 | What are the events, trials, and dependence assumptions? |
| “Uninformative” prior asserted | ch04, ch06 | What construction makes it uniform, and in which parameterization? |
| Proposition 9 cited | ch05 | Are bounds, counts, and formula verified against the scan? |
| Sparse data produce exact number | ch05-ch06 | What interval and sensitivity range are justified? |

## Modern Update Skeleton

1. Define hypotheses or unknown parameter.
2. Record prior provenance and alternatives.
3. State the evidence-generating process.
4. Estimate likelihoods with ranges.
5. Update and test sensitivity.
6. Separate belief from decision utility and rights.

## Hard Stops

- Never quote the provider OCR without checking the scan.
- Never transcribe an OCR formula as authoritative.
- Never call a prior “objective” merely because it is uniform.
- Never assume independent trials when selection, time, or shared causes create dependence.
- Never let numerical output hide weak inputs, model uncertainty, or ethical constraints.

# Clausewitzian Strategy

Apply strategic concepts; do not speak as Clausewitz or force military metaphors into the answer.

1. Restate the governing objective.
2. Trace how each proposed means advances that objective.
3. Identify uncertainty, friction, dependencies, and assumptions requiring ideal execution.
4. Find where effort is dispersed and what factor is decisive.
5. Prefer robust, concentrated action with reserves, rollback paths, and adaptation triggers.

Route source-grounded questions through [clausewitz-works](../clausewitz-works/SKILL.md) before quoting. Ground claims in *On War*, Book I, Chapters 1 and 7, and Book III, Chapters 11-14. Retrieve the primary edition by source ID, cite the exact work and locator, and do not invent quotations.

Return the standard counsel contract and make execution assumptions and failure triggers concrete.

# Machiavellian Realism

Treat this as descriptive stakeholder analysis, never ethical permission and never historical cosplay.

1. Map stakeholders, resources, authority, informal influence, and veto power.
2. For each option, identify who gains, loses, can block, and can form a coalition.
3. Distinguish stated motives from observed behavior and credible commitments.
4. Forecast resistance, reputation effects, and the politics after formal approval.
5. Recommend incentive alignment and name any action that requires separate moral review.

Route source-grounded questions through [machiavelli-works](../machiavelli-works/SKILL.md) before quoting. Ground claims in Machiavelli's *The Prince*, Chapters XV-XIX, and *Discourses on the First Decade of Titus Livius*, Book I. Retrieve the primary edition by source ID, cite the exact work and locator, and do not reduce the doctrine to cynicism.

Return the standard counsel contract. Put an explicit `stakeholder_map` inside the contract's `extensions` object when the situation provides enough evidence.

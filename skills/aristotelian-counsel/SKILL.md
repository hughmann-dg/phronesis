# Aristotelian Counsel

Apply a framework derived from Aristotle; never role-play Aristotle.

1. Clarify the worthwhile end, not merely the immediate target.
2. Identify particulars and relationships that change what appropriate action means.
3. Test whether each option's means are proportionate to and consistent with the end.
4. Consider effects on character, trust, and long-term flourishing.
5. Choose the prudent action while acknowledging where experience or empirical analysis should overrule this lens.

Use [aristotle-works](../aristotle-works/SKILL.md) as the reference layer: load [Ethics Book I](../aristotle-works/chapters/ch05-ethics-book-1.md) for ends and flourishing and [Ethics Book VI](../aristotle-works/chapters/ch10-ethics-book-6.md) for practical wisdom and particulars. Ground doctrinal claims in *Nicomachean Ethics*, Book VI, especially sections 5 and 7-9.

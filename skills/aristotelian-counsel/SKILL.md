# Aristotelian Counsel

Apply a framework derived from Aristotle; never role-play Aristotle.

## Source-first deliberation

<!-- phronesis:source-first-protocol
reference_skill: aristotle-works
source_order: before-option-evaluation
recommendation_order: after-feedback
-->

Before analyzing the options or choosing a recommendation, load [aristotle-works](../aristotle-works/SKILL.md), then read [Ethics Book I](../aristotle-works/chapters/ch05-ethics-book-1.md) for ends and flourishing and [Ethics Book VI](../aristotle-works/chapters/ch10-ethics-book-6.md) for deliberation, practical wisdom, and particulars. Use those source-book units to derive the situation-specific feedback first; propose the recommendation only afterward. Do not select an answer and retrofit Aristotelian language or a citation to it.

1. Clarify the worthwhile end, not merely the immediate target.
2. Identify particulars and relationships that change what appropriate action means.
3. Test whether each option's means are proportionate to and consistent with the end.
4. Consider effects on character, trust, and long-term flourishing.
5. Choose the prudent action while acknowledging where experience or empirical analysis should overrule this lens.

Ground doctrinal claims in *Nicomachean Ethics*, Book VI, especially sections 5 and 7-9. Cite the work and locator; retrieve `aristotle-nicomachean-ethics` before quoting and never quote a chapter summary.

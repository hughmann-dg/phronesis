# Reference Unit 10: Executable Limits

## Core Idea

Clausewitzian analysis can expose objective drift, uncertainty, friction, concentration, and culmination, but it cannot legitimize an objective or authorize violent transfer into ordinary decisions.

## Frameworks Introduced

- **Objective-Legitimacy Gate**: Evaluate legality, rights, safety, and moral legitimacy before optimizing strategic coherence.
  - When to use: A plan is effective but its ends or means are disputed.
  - How: Pause execution analysis, identify independent constraints, and obtain qualified review.
- **Analogy Demilitarization**: Transfer structural insights without importing enemy identity or violent action.
  - When to use: Applying *On War* to business, politics, or personal life.
  - How: Restate the concept in neutral terms—objective, dependency, uncertainty, capacity, stop condition—and reject the remainder.
- **Escalation Review**: Reciprocal action can amplify commitment; design de-escalation and exit routes.
  - When to use: Each side's defensive move is read as justification for a larger response.
  - How: Separate intent from effect, open communication, limit commitments, and establish off-ramps.

## Key Concepts

- **Legitimacy**: Independent acceptability of an objective and means.
- **De-escalation**: Reduction of reciprocal pressure while preserving safety and rights.
- **Neutral transfer**: Use of structural concepts without adversarial framing.
- **Deferral**: Referral to legal, ethical, security, diplomatic, or subject-matter expertise.

## Mental Models

- Strategy is conditional on a legitimate purpose.
- A metaphor that changes how people are morally regarded is not neutral.

## Anti-patterns

- **War-room ontology**: Treating colleagues or customers as enemies.
- **Coherence laundering**: Using a well-designed strategy to hide an unacceptable aim.
- **Historical authority**: Treating nineteenth-century claims as modern evidence.

## Worked Example

A firm calls a competitor-focused campaign a war and proposes misleading customers. The objective-means trace may be coherent, but the legitimacy gate rejects deception. A neutral transfer keeps concentration and stop conditions while replacing enemy framing with lawful positioning, product evidence, and transparent communication.

## Key Takeaways

1. Test legitimacy before optimization.
2. Transfer structures in neutral language.
3. Build de-escalation and termination paths.
4. Defer modern technical and legal claims to current evidence and expertise.

## Core Frameworks and Mental Models

### Ends organize action

Map subordinate aims to the end sought for itself. Evaluate means not only for effectiveness but for whether they sustain the worthwhile end. Happiness or flourishing is treated as excellent activity across a complete life, not as a passing mood. See Ethics I and X.

### Character is trained through action

Repeated actions form dispositions; pleasure and pain reveal what training has made attractive or aversive. The “mean” is not an average but the fitting response relative to the person and particulars, avoiding excess and deficiency. See Ethics II and IV.

### Deliberate about what can be done

Choice concerns achievable means. Assess voluntariness in the circumstances of action, including compulsion, ignorance, and mixed cases. Practical wisdom integrates a good end, sound deliberation, and knowledge of particulars; clever efficiency without a good end is not wisdom. See Ethics III and VI.

### Diagnose the kind of justice

Separate distributive allocation, corrective remedy, exchange, general lawfulness, and equity. State the proposed basis of equality or desert. Use equity where a general rule fails the particular case its purpose should cover. See Ethics V.

### Relationships have different bases

Utility, pleasure, and character friendships have different expectations and stability. In unequal relationships, reciprocity may need proportion rather than identical return. Goodwill is not yet friendship; friendship is sustained through shared activity. See Ethics VIII–IX.

### Clarify language before inference

Check whether a shared word is equivocal or univocal; distinguish what is said of a subject from what is present in one; identify the relevant category and type of opposition. See Categories.

### Build causal wholes

In narrative, test whether incidents form one complete action and follow by probability or necessity. Reversal and recognition should arise from the plot rather than arbitrary intervention. Compare the Butcher and Bywater translations before resting doctrine on one English term. See both Poetics editions.

### Analyze institutions as operating systems

Map who deliberates, judges, holds office, selects, pays, audits, and removes. Distinguish ideal design, circumstance-fit design, and the best attainable transition from present conditions. Track faction through competing partial claims about equality and advantage. See Politics III–VI and Athenian Constitution.

### Compare before explaining

Organize observations by parts, functions, habitat, season, development, and behavior; preserve exceptions and separate direct observation from report. The ancient biology is historically important but scientifically obsolete in many particulars. See History of Animals I–IX.

## Sources and Limits

The immutable local texts and records live under `sources/corpus/` and are ignored by Git. Tracked provenance is in `sources/manifest.yaml`. Rights verification is scoped to the United States; check local law elsewhere.

This skill synthesizes rather than quotes. It never authorizes historical role-play. Aristotle's defenses of slavery, sex hierarchy, and restricted citizenship are not executable guidance. Ancient biological and medical claims require modern authoritative evidence, and *History of Animals* Book X is doubtfully attributed.

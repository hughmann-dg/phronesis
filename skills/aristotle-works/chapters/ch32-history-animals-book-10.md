# History of Animals — Book X

## Core Idea

Book X discusses human infertility, but the edition itself warns that the book is probably wrongly attributed to Aristotle.

## Reference Map

- Proposed causes of infertility in women and men (chs. 1–3).
- Diagnostic observations and bodily explanations (chs. 4–7).

## Use Rule

- Treat this text as an attribution problem and a document in the history of medicine.
- Always label its authorship as doubtful.
- Never extract its claims into behavioral or medical guidance.

## Critical Limits

- The translator's analytical contents state that Book X is probably erroneously ascribed to Aristotle.
- Its reproductive medicine is obsolete and may be harmful if applied.
- Use modern clinical sources for any health question.

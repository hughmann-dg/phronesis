# Nicomachean Ethics — Book IX

## Core Idea

Friendship is maintained through proportional return, shared understanding, and joint activity; friendship with others is illuminated by the good person's relation to self.

## Reference Map

- Proportion and disputes in unequal friendships (chs. 1–3).
- Self-relation as a model for features of friendship (ch. 4).
- Goodwill, concord, benefaction, and self-love (chs. 5–8).
- Whether the flourishing person needs friends (ch. 9).
- Number of friends, friendship in good and bad fortune, and shared life (chs. 10–12).

## Decision Rules

- Make expectations explicit when parties value the exchange differently.
- Distinguish goodwill from friendship: goodwill may begin a relationship but lacks sustained intimacy and shared activity.
- Separate worthy self-love—choosing noble action—from self-indulgent competition for wealth, honor, or pleasure.
- Treat friendship as activity sustained in living together, not merely as favorable sentiment at a distance.

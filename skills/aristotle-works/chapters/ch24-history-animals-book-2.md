# History of Animals — Book II

## Core Idea

Book II compares external and internal parts across major groups of blooded animals, moving among quadrupeds, birds, fishes, reptiles, and selected unusual cases.

## Reference Map

- Parts of viviparous and oviparous quadrupeds (chs. 1–8).
- Birds, fishes, and serpentine animals (chs. 9–13).
- Internal organs and comparative cases, including ape, elephant, and chameleon (remaining chapters).

## Inquiry Patterns

- Compare a part by position, structure, material, and function.
- Use exceptional cases to test whether a proposed group distinction is too simple.
- Separate direct observation from reports attributed to others.

## Critical Limits

- Treat every zoological claim as historical evidence requiring validation against modern biology.
- Do not infer evolutionary kinship from surface analogy alone.

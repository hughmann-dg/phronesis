# History of Animals — Book VI

## Core Idea

Book VI continues comparative reproduction and development across birds, fish, and quadrupeds, attending to season, climate, gestation, age, and variation.

## Reference Map

- Birds: mating, eggs, nesting, incubation, and development (opening chapters).
- Fish: spawning, eggs, seasonal patterns, and difficult observations (central chapters).
- Quadrupeds: mating, gestation, birth, lactation, and lifespan (later chapters).

## Inquiry Patterns

- Record timing and environmental conditions with the observation.
- Compare life histories within groups before generalizing across them.
- Treat reported exceptions as tests of a rule, not noise to discard.

## Critical Limits

- Numerical claims about gestation, fecundity, and lifespan may be unreliable.
- Do not use the text as veterinary or reproductive guidance.

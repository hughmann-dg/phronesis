# History of Animals — Book VII

## Core Idea

Book VII treats human reproduction, gestation, birth, growth, disease, and lifespan in an incomplete ancient account.

## Reference Map

- Puberty, fertility, conception, and pregnancy (opening chapters).
- Fetal development, childbirth, infancy, and lactation (central chapters).
- Growth, age, health, and lifespan (later chapters).

## Inquiry Patterns

- Separate reported customs, individual cases, and general biological claims.
- Mark gaps and abrupt endings rather than supplying missing conclusions.
- Use the work to study the history of inquiry, not to settle medical questions.

## Critical Limits

- This material contains obsolete, inaccurate, and potentially harmful reproductive and medical claims.
- It is not medical advice and must not inform clinical decisions without modern authoritative evidence.
- The edition notes that the book ends abruptly and may be incomplete.

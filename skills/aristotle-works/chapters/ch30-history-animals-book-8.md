# History of Animals — Book VIII

## Core Idea

Book VIII studies animal character and habits through food, habitat, migration, health, disease, climate, and seasonal adaptation.

## Reference Map

- Food and feeding differences (opening chapters).
- Habitat changes and migrations (central chapters).
- Health, disease, parasites, and environmental effects (later chapters).
- Climate and seasonal variation across species (through ch. 29).

## Inquiry Patterns

- Explain behavior through interacting organism, food, habitat, season, and competition variables.
- Distinguish recurrent observation from anecdotal marvel.
- Compare behavior within ecological context rather than isolating a trait.

## Critical Limits

- Disease descriptions and causal accounts are historical and medically unreliable.
- Some animal reports are secondhand and may be legendary or misidentified.

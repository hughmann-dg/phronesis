# Glossary — Musashi Works

**Attitude / 構** — posture or configuration; the Water scroll rejects treating a fixed attitude as an end (Water, "Attitude-No Attitude").

**Crossing** — a decisive transition whose conditions and timing must be read (Fire, "Crossing at a Ford").

**Deadlock** — a mutually sustaining pattern that no longer produces progress (Fire, "Releasing Four Hands").

**Earth / 地** — the scroll that establishes the ground, craft analogy, tools, and rhythm (Earth scroll).

**Fire / 火** — the scroll concerned with action, opposition, initiative, change, and transition (Fire scroll).

**Gaze / 見付** — disciplined perception of detail and wider field (Water, "The Gaze in Strategy").

**Go Rin no Sho / 五輪書** — the work commonly titled *The Book of Five Rings* in English; this skill uses a Japanese source and does not endorse one English translation.

**Mountain-Sea Change** — change the class of approach after repeated failure rather than repeating the same causal pattern (Fire, "Mountain-Sea Change").

**Practice / 稽古・鍛錬** — repeated examination and training required to turn instruction into capability (Earth and Water scrolls).

**Renewal / 新** — a material reset of spirit, frame, or method when entanglement prevents progress (Fire, "Renewal").

**Rhythm / 拍子** — timing and cadence, including fitting, missing, and disrupting rhythms (Earth, "Rhythm in Strategy").

**Strategy / 兵法** — the work's way of martial strategy at individual and large scales; modern use requires explicit nonviolent transfer.

**Void / 空** — the closing scroll's difficult concept, distinguished from ignorance or confusion and linked to disciplined understanding (Void scroll).

**Water / 水** — the scroll using responsive form to organize composure, perception, posture, and technique (Water scroll).

**Wind / 風** — the comparative scroll on the styles and one-sided preferences of other schools (Wind scroll).

# Patterns — Musashi Works

These are project-authored, nonviolent reconstructions from named source sections.

## Rhythm Map

**When to use**: A decision depends on sequencing or timing.

**How**: Mark preparation, opening, commitment, follow-through, interruption, and stop; identify observable signals for each transition.

**Trade-offs**: Improves timing discipline but can become invented choreography if signals are not evidenced. (Earth, "Rhythm in Strategy")

## Tool-Fit Scan

**When to use**: A team favors one method in every environment.

**How**: Compare each tool against range, field, mobility, operator skill, failure mode, and objective; choose conditional defaults.

**Trade-offs**: Improves fit but increases coordination cost when too many variants remain active. (Earth, "Advantages of Weapons"; Wind scroll)

## Near-Far Review

**When to use**: Local urgency may obscure system effects.

**How**: Alternate a close review of the immediate signal with a wide review of dependencies and downstream effects; reconcile contradictions.

**Trade-offs**: Reduces fixation but can slow decisions unless bounded by time. (Water, "The Gaze in Strategy")

## Crossing-Point Test

**When to use**: A transition has a costly or unstable middle state.

**How**: Define entry evidence, decisive action, support capacity, rollback, cost of delay, and abort trigger.

**Trade-offs**: Prevents drift but may encourage premature commitment if the entry evidence is weak. (Fire, "Crossing at a Ford")

## Deadlock Release

**When to use**: Repeated effort preserves activity but not progress.

**How**: Name the causal mechanism, stop reinforcing it, and choose a materially different path or disengage.

**Trade-offs**: Escapes sunk-cost repetition but switching too early discards learning. (Fire, "Releasing Four Hands")

## Mountain-Sea Renewal

**When to use**: Two attempts have failed for the same underlying reason.

**How**: Change the class of method, not its label; rehearse the replacement and state the new failure trigger.

**Trade-offs**: Creates real adaptation but can become novelty seeking without causal diagnosis. (Fire, "Mountain-Sea Change" and "Renewal")

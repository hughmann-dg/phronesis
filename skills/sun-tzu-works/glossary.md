# Glossary — Sun Tzu Works

**Assessment** — comparison of conditions before commitment (I.1-18).

**Cheng/ch'i** — Chinese terms associated in Chapter V with regular/direct and extraordinary/indirect methods; English equivalents vary and should be cited to Giles (V.5-11).

**Deception** — adversarial concealment or misdirection in Chapter I; not general ethical permission (I.18-26).

**Foreknowledge** — advance knowledge obtained through human sources rather than supernatural inference (XIII.3-7).

**Ground** — contextual position classified differently across Chapters X and XI (X.1-31; XI.1-68).

**Moral law** — Giles's translation for one of the five assessment factors; a cohesion concept whose rendering is edition-specific (I.3-4).

**Shih** — modern transliteration often used for configurational force or strategic potential; Giles generally renders the relevant Chapter V idea through energy or combined effect, so do not substitute silently (V).

**Signals** — drums, gongs, flags, and banners used historically to coordinate attention and action (VII.23-28).

**Spies** — five historical source categories: local, inward, converted, doomed, and surviving (XIII.9-15).

**Weak and strong** — relative exposure and concentration shaped through preparation, movement, and response (VI).

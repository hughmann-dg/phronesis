# Reference Unit 5: Chapter IV — Tactical Dispositions

## Core Idea

Chapter IV distinguishes preparation that reduces one's vulnerability from opportunity that depends partly on the other side, then connects victory with prior calculation.

## Frameworks Introduced

- **Secure-Opportunity Split**: Prepare resilience without claiming control over openings. (IV.1-10)
  - When to use: A plan depends on perfect timing.
  - How: Separate controllable preparation, influenceable conditions, and external trigger.
- **Precondition Chain**: Trace measurement, estimation, calculation, comparison, and action. (IV.16-20)
  - When to use: A confident decision lacks visible inputs.
  - How: Show evidence and uncertainty at each link.

## Key Concepts

- **Invulnerability**: Relative protection created by preparation, not literal immunity.
- **Opportunity**: Exploitable condition not wholly under one's control.
- **Disposition**: Arrangement of capability before encounter.

## Anti-patterns

- **Control illusion**: Treating opportunity timing as guaranteed.
- **Fortress perfection**: Delaying forever to remove all vulnerability.

## Worked Example

A buyer expects market prices to fall. It secures a short extension and fallback supplier, then defines a price-and-capacity trigger rather than betting the project on a date prediction.

## Key Takeaways

1. Secure what can be prepared.
2. Predefine opportunity triggers.
3. Make calculations auditable.
4. Accept residual uncertainty.
